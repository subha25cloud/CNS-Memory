### **Strategy 1: Multi-Timeframe Scalping with EMA, RSI, and Bollinger Bands**  
- **Concept:** Use large timeframe (Weekly/Monthly) RSI + BB zones to trigger scalps on lower timeframes (1m–5m)  
- **Entry Logic:**  
  - Monthly RSI > 80 + BB breakout  
  - 5-min candle closes below 20EMA + wick + volume  
- **Exit Logic:** 1:2–1:3 RR or reversal candle  
- **ML Role:** Detect overbought zones, wick exhaustion, and volume anomaly

---

### **Strategy 2: RSI Cluster & Candlestick Trap Reversal**  
- **Concept:** Trap-based reversal scalping after RSI > 80 or < 20  
- **Entry Logic:**  
  - Rejection wick candle post RSI extreme  
  - Entry on next candle with tight SL  
- **ML Role:** Detect fakeouts + wick trap + RSI zone clusters

---

### **Strategy 3: EMA Reversal Bounce (5 EMA Non-Touch Setup)**  
- **Concept:** Trend exhaustion reversal when price fails to touch 5EMA  
- **Entry Logic:**  
  - Strong trend without EMA touch  
  - Entry when reversal candle breaks prior low/high  
- **ML Role:** Detect “non-touch” runs and confirm reversal pressure

---

### **Strategy 4: Weekly EMA Bounce + Intraday Trigger**  
- **Concept:** Use Weekly EMA (50/100) bounce zone to trigger intraday scalps  
- **Entry Logic:**  
  - Price bounces at EMA on Weekly  
  - Confirmation on 5/15min chart with wick or inside bar  
- **ML Role:** Detect EMA zone proximity + intraday trigger sync
